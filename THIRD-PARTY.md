# Third-party material

## Fonts

All three families are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose text for
each is beside the files in `static/fonts/`.

- **Spectral** (Production Type), `spectral-*.woff2`: subsets made by
  levineuwirth.org's `tools/subset-fonts.py`, which keep the small capitals,
  oldstyle figures and ligatures. Its license reserves no font name, so a
  subset may carry it. License: `OFL-Spectral.txt`.
- **JetBrains Mono** (JetBrains), `jetbrains-mono-*.woff2`: subsets made the
  same way. Its license reserves no font name. License: `OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt`.
- **Fira Sans** (The Mozilla Foundation and Telefonica S.A.),
  `fira-sans-regular.woff2` and `fira-sans-semibold.woff2`. The license
  shipped with Google's copy reserves no font name, but the upstream
  release's license reserves "Fira" (levineuwirth.org's copy says so). So
  these are not subsets, which keeps them sound under either. They are the
  unmodified `FiraSans-Regular.ttf` and `FiraSans-SemiBold.ttf` from
  `https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/firasans`, read on
  2026-10-27, re-encoded as WOFF2 with no other change. The source files'
  SHA-256 sums:
  - `FiraSans-Regular.ttf`: `c29556a2719bf613ef3d5e070e40d903a8965d9c081beca1375dc1e6e0f93c23`
  - `FiraSans-SemiBold.ttf`: `db0321f83eb3e9f527b8af384a1b3fefdc1039cf2b06fd39b3f61492bda9561c`

  License: `OFL-FiraSans.txt`, from the same directory.
